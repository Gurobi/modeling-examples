Gurobi Modeling Examples
========================

A collection of self-contained modelling examples that show how to formulate and
solve optimization problems with ``gurobipy``.

Each example is a Jupyter notebook covering a realistic use case: the physical or
business background, the mathematical formulation, the ``gurobipy`` model, and a
discussion of the solution. Together they span linear and mixed-integer programs,
quadratic and quadratically constrained programs, and nonlinear models, across a
range of industries and difficulty levels.

Head over to the :doc:`gallery` to browse the examples by model type, industry or
difficulty.

.. note::

   While these examples are free to use, the ``gurobipy`` library they depend on
   is commercial software and requires a license. A `limited size license
   <https://www.gurobi.com/features/web-license-service/>`_ is included with the
   ``gurobipy`` package and is enough to run most of the examples.
