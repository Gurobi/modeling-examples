Marketing Campaign Optimization
===============================

.. image:: thumbnails/marketing-campaign-optimization.jpg
   :width: 600
   :align: center
   :alt: Marketing Campaign Optimization

This example determines which products to offer to individual customers in order to maximize total expected profit while satisfying business constraints. The problem is common in the banking and financial services industry and is formulated as a mixed-integer optimization model.
