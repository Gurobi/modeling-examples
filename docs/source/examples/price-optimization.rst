Avocado Price Optimization
==========================

.. image:: thumbnails/price-optimization.jpg
   :width: 600
   :align: center
   :alt: Avocado Price Optimization

The avocado pricing and supply problem offers opportunities to combine predictive and prescriptive analytics. This example uses regression to model demand and optimization to set the avocado prices that maximize revenue. The notebooks cover both parts of the workflow.
