Optimize a Power Generation Schedule
====================================

.. image:: thumbnails/power-generation.jpg
   :width: 600
   :align: center
   :alt: Optimize a Power Generation Schedule

This example generates a power schedule for the U.S. state of Georgia. It decides how much power each plant generates each hour, and when to switch plants on and off, so that the hourly demand is met at minimum overall cost. The problem is a mixed-integer program.
