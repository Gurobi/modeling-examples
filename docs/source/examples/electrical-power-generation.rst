Electrical Power Generation
===========================

.. image:: thumbnails/electrical-power-generation.jpg
   :width: 600
   :align: center
   :alt: Electrical Power Generation

This example, also known as the unit commitment problem, selects which power stations to turn on in order to satisfy the anticipated power demand over a 24-hour horizon at minimum cost. The problem is formulated as a mixed-integer program.
