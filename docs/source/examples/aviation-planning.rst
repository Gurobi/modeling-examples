Airline Planning After Flight Disruption
========================================

.. image:: thumbnails/aviation-planning.jpg
   :width: 600
   :align: center
   :alt: Airline Planning After Flight Disruption

Weather events such as snowstorms and heavy rain force airlines to decide which flights to operate and which to cancel. This example formulates that decision as a mixed-integer optimization problem that helps aviation planners recover their schedules after a weather disruption.
