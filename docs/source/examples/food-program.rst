Food Program
============

.. image:: thumbnails/food-program.jpg
   :width: 600
   :align: center
   :alt: Food Program

The United Nations World Food Programme supplies food assistance to around 100 million people in 80 countries each year. This example builds an optimization model that sets up a food supply chain using real World Food Programme data, transporting food from supply cities to the areas where it is needed.
