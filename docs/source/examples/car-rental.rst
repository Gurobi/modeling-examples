Car Rental
==========

.. image:: thumbnails/car-rental.jpg
   :width: 600
   :align: center
   :alt: Car Rental

A car rental company must decide how many cars to own and where to position them at the start of each day of the week to maximize weekly profit. The first example formulates the problem as a linear program, and the second adds binary decision variables.
