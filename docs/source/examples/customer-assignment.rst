Customer Assignment Problem
===========================

.. image:: thumbnails/customer-assignment.jpg
   :width: 600
   :align: center
   :alt: Customer Assignment Problem

Companies must decide where to build facilities such as factories, warehouses, and hospitals to support their operations. This example models how customers are assigned to these facilities as a mixed-integer program that balances facility costs against service requirements.
