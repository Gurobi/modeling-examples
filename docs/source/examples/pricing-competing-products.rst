Price Optimization with Competing Products
==========================================

.. image:: thumbnails/pricing-competing-products.jpg
   :width: 600
   :align: center
   :alt: Price Optimization with Competing Products

This example decides how to divide a limited amount of space among competing product categories and which prices to set, in order to maximize revenue. The model is a quadratic program that includes a quadratic penalty on prices.
