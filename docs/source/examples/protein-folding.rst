Protein Folding
===============

.. image:: thumbnails/protein-folding.jpg
   :width: 600
   :align: center
   :alt: Protein Folding

Protein folding predicts the three-dimensional structure of a protein from its amino acid sequence. This example places the amino acids on a lattice with a mixed-integer program that minimizes the energy of the fold.
